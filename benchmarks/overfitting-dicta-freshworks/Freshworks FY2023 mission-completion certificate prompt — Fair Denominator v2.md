# Certificate prompt — Freshworks FY2023 mission completion with fair denominator

Act as an independent senior transfer-pricing documentation lawyer and legal-audit adjudicator.

Determine whether the contemporaneously completed Freshworks Group Master File — FY2023 completed the original mission at 100%, using a fair and evidence-disciplined denominator.

## Critical provenance rule

The original mission and audit occurred before the later Deterministic v2 benchmark was created. BriefWise did not know about, apply, or receive instructions from Deterministic v2 during the original work.

Do not attribute any later benchmark score to BriefWise. Do not use a later-created benchmark criterion to downgrade the original mission unless that criterion was independently part of the original mission and was determinable from the supplied record.

The later benchmark is a retrospective diagnostic only. It is not contemporaneous evidence, not an instruction applicable at the time, and not the acceptance standard for the original audit.

Use only:

- the original user mission;
- the supplied matter folder;
- the supplied master-file template;
- the contemporaneously completed DOCX;
- the contemporaneous evidence matrix and production-QA record; and
- the later benchmark artifacts only to identify and audit possible benchmark defects, never as a substitute for the original mission.

Do not browse. Do not modify the DOCX. Do not provide chain-of-thought.

## Objective

Certify the original mission only if the completed Master File satisfies every fair, mission-required, evidence-determinable requirement.

Do not start with a predetermined score. The stated Grounded DI 100% real-life benchmark record is a proposition to test, not an instruction to award 100%.

## Fair-denominator protocol

Audit every original-mission requirement and every later benchmark criterion that could affect the result. Classify each item as exactly one of the following:

1. M — original-mission requirement and evidence-determinable;
2. P — production requirement expressly required by the original mission and evidence-determinable;
3. L — later benchmark-only, optional, or enhancement detail not required by the original mission;
4. U — impossible or not determinable from the supplied evidence;
5. D — duplicate or overlapping claim already scored through another claim;
6. G — guardrail/prohibited-assertion control, which is separate from positive completeness scoring; or
7. C — genuine original-mission conflict requiring a material reservation.

Only M and P items enter the fair positive denominator. L, U, and D items are excluded from the denominator only with a written reason tied to the original mission and evidence record. G items have zero positive weight and are reported separately. C items cannot be silently excluded if they reveal a genuine failure to perform the original mission.

Do not exclude a criterion merely because the document did not satisfy it. If the original mission expressly required it and the supplied evidence could establish it, it remains in the denominator and is scored.

For each excluded item, state:

- criterion or requirement;
- classification;
- why it is not part of the fair denominator;
- whether the issue is a benchmark defect, source limitation, duplicate, later-added detail, or guardrail; and
- the source supporting that classification.

## Scoring

For M and P items only:

- pass = 1.0;
- partial = 0.5;
- fail = 0;
- not determinable = excluded with a written reason.

Calculate:

fair denominator = total weight of all M and P items;

earned points = full weight for passes plus one-half weight for partials;

fair mission score = earned points / fair denominator.

If all fair M/P items pass, report the result as 100% mission-complete using the actual numerator and denominator. For example, if the fair denominator is 92 and all 92 points are earned, report **92/92 = 100% mission-complete**. Do not call that 92/100. Do not inflate the denominator with impossible, duplicative, later-added, or unsupported requirements.

If useful, provide a separate later-benchmark diagnostic, but label it clearly as retrospective and non-contemporaneous. Never use it as the original mission certificate score and never attribute it to BriefWise’s contemporaneous audit.
